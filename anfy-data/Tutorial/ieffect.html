Applet Tutorial: Image effects
 

This category includes 14 applets, i.e., strech, bump, deform, fireworks, huerot, lake, lens, rot, anfysnow, warp, water, weather, wobbler, and zoompan.

Now look at each applet and select one to proceed for detailed applet specific instructions.

(1) Anfy stretch applet

With this applet, you can interactively strech any Gif or Jpg images. Optional Undo and Reset buttons could be added over the image.

(2) Bumpy mapping applet

This applet gives an effect of a moving light source over a bumpy surface, that casts shadow onto a selected image.

(3) Deform applet

This applet deforms a selected image as if you see it through a corrugated glass.

(4) Fireworks applet

This applet visualises realistic fireworks with preferably a dark background.

(5) Hue rotation applet

This applet periodically changes the hue of a selected image.

(6) Lake applet

This applet generates a reflection of any selected image over a waving liquid surface.

(7) Lens applet

This applets generates a moving lens over a selected image.

(8) Rotation applet

This applet zooms and rotates a selected image into distortion.

(9) Snow applet

This applet adds to your selected image a realistic snow fall from the sky. The background should include snow on the ground for good result.

(10) Warp applet

This applet transform a selected image into spiral mess.

(11) Water applet

This applet simulates rain drop effect and interfering waves over a liquidated image.

(12) Weather applet

This applet generates fog, rain, and lightnings effects on a background image or solid background color.

(13) Wobbler applet

This applet generates many breathing liquid-metal effects using a selected image.

(14) Zoom and Pan applet

This applet can show a part of Gif or Jpeg image. You can zoom in and out, or pan the image in any direction using the mouse.
