
                  Domain Detect Utility by Fabio Ciucci


Usage:


Copy the domain.class and domain.html files to the web site where you
plan to place the Anfy applets. Then, load domain.html with your 
Java-capable browser.

You can have one of those 3 responses from the applet:

1) Sorry, your browser doesn't support Java

   Literally, your browser doesn't support Java(tm)!!


2) NO DOMAIN DETECTED! NO REGCODE REQUIRED!

   This means that the applet is run locally and no regcodes are required.


3) You will see the name of the domain, and you are required to purchase
the appropriate regcode from us.
