Age:"Thirty-Something"
-------------------------
Actually I'm only thirty (at the time of producing this Bio) but my birthday is September 25, (anyone wanting to send me a Birthday Present is welcome to do so, I won't complain...a new laptop would be ideal!) and I was born in 1965, so when you are reading this you should be able to figure out how old I am.
Some people are leary about putting too much info about themselves on the net, but I look at it this way: If someone wants to know something about you, they'll find a way. I don't really feel thirty. More like twenty-something. When I'm on a creative bent, you could probably subtract another 10-15 years. I feel I'm at the perfect age right now. Why? Sorry, I can't answer that...this is the "WHO" section. But I can tell you that some people think I am older than this for a couple of reasons. 1) I have some grey hair. Okay, not some. A lot. I like my grey hair...kinda Shaun Connery-ish. Better than Picard-ish. 2)Some people say that I act mature for thirty...is this possible? I mean, from the text you're reading, do you get that feeling. Nah! But then again I do loosen up a lot at the keyboard...
