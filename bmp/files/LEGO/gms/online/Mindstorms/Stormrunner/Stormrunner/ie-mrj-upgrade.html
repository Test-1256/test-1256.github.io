UPGRADE REQUIRED!

You'll need the most recent version of Internet Explorer and Apple's Java classes to play STORMRUNNER.

Click here to get the newest Internet Explorer and install it if you do not already have it. Since you are on a Mac, you must use Internet Explorer to play Stormrunner.

Then click here to get Apple's newest version of the MRJ. Once you have downloaded it, install it, and then go into your Internet Explorer preferences (under the Edit menu). Under Java, select the "Apple MRJ" as your Java Virtual Machine, then quit and re-open your browser, come back and try again!
